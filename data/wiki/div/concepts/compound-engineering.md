# Compound Engineering

A philosophy of software engineering for the AI age that features a loop of Planning, Working, Reviewing, and Compounding to create self-improving AI coding systems.

## Key Facts
- The core compound engineering loop consists of four key steps: Plan, Work, Review, and Compound, with an optional Brainstorming step.
