# Distributed Systems

A distributed system is a computing environment in which various components are spread across multiple computers (or other computing devices) on a network. These devices split up the work, coordinating their efforts to complete the job more efficiently or reliably.

## Key Facts
- **Challenges**: Involves complex challenges such as network partitions, state synchronization, consensus, fault tolerance, and message delays.
- **Consistency vs. Availability**: Systems often have to trade off between strong consistency and high availability (as described by the CAP theorem).

## Connections
